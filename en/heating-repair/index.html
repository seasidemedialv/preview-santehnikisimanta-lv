The LV portal explains that post-war apartment blocks mostly have a one-pipe vertical system: radiators receive heat in sequence, and the flow in the riser is maintained by a bypass at each radiator. Private houses by Kooperatīva iela and in Kleisti have their own circuit with a heating boiler.

Radiator, floor and heating boiler in Imanta buildings

A radiator on the 8th floor of a 602-series block

In a nine-storey block, radiators on a single one-pipe riser are on every floor, and the water passes through them one after another. If a radiator or valve is replaced on the 8th floor, the building manager has to shut off the riser and drain it; all the floors on that riser are without heat at the time. The work is planned with the building manager; more on the page radiator replacement.

Underfloor heating at Dumbrāju iela 29 and in a panel-block flat next door

In 2022 the project “Vertikāles” was handed over at Dumbrāju iela 29: two buildings with 134 flats where underfloor heating was installed per the design. A panel-block flat does not have it. The Ministry of Economics explained in an LV portal consultation that connecting underfloor heating to the building’s heating without the proper documentation qualifies as unauthorised construction. What can be done is explained on the page water underfloor heating installation.

A heating boiler in a private house by Kooperatīva iela

On both sides of Kooperatīva iela the private-house district took shape in the late 1940s and in the 1950s, and each house has its own heating circuit. When assembling it anew or in parts, the seals matter: IMI Heimeier states that mineral oil or lubricants containing mineral oil in heating water usually cause EPDM seals to disintegrate. So when reassembling old valves and joints, such lubricants are not used in the circuit.

How much water goes through the radiator and how much past it

In a one-pipe riser part of the water goes through the radiator and part through the bypass. For its E-Z manifold IMI Heimeier states that in one-pipe mode the flow to the radiator can be set from 30 to 60% (35% at the factory). The bypass stays open even if the radiator is shut off, so that circulation in the riser is not interrupted.

The whole building is regulated elsewhere. In a Danfoss example in Riga, Zvaigznāju gatve 6 (5 storeys, 155 radiators, 19 risers), the one-pipe system was modernised only in the basement, by installing pressure-independent balancing valves on the risers, and the radiator connections were not changed. The recalculated consumption fell from 200.58 to 174.44 MWh per season. That is a decision for the building, not the work of one flat.

For a flat one practical point follows: a radiator valve or manifold is replaced so that the bypass stays open. If there is no bypass or it is closed, shutting off the radiator stops the flow throughout the riser, not only in your room. So before the work, check what connection your radiator has and whether there is a bypass at all.

Drain or not: that changes the amount

In heating work one question changes the price most: whether the riser or circuit has to be drained. If a dripping valve can be fixed without draining the riser, the line “Finding and fixing a leak” from €40 per job applies. Radiator replacement is from €75 each, and for that job the building manager shuts off the riser, or in a house the owner shuts off the circuit.

For example: in a 464-series flat a heated towel rail is replaced in the same place and the seal of a dripping radiator valve is redone at the same time. Heated towel rail replacement from €80 each and leak fixing from €40 per job, in total at least €120, labour only, materials extra. Whether travel will be a separate line on the invoice, the plumber tells you on the call, before anything is unscrewed.

What residents ask

What does the Imanta heat plant's electrode boiler change in my flat?

In February 2026 AS “Rīgas siltums” installed a 49 MW electrode water heating boiler at the “Imanta” heat plant, and the company stated that in summer it could supply hot water to the whole of Imanta. The radiators, risers and heat substation in your building do not change because of it: Rīgas siltums reminds that heat substations and internal heating systems are the common property of the flat owners.

In October the radiator is warm here and cool there. Is that a fault?

Not always. Rīgas siltums explains that an automated heat substation reacts to the outdoor temperature, so a changing radiator temperature means the substation is working. In 2023 LSM reported that in Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks buildings the supply is cut off at +12 °C, and after it is switched on the system can take two weeks to stabilise, in some cases up to four weeks.

Should the radiator thermostat be turned off when airing a room?

Rīgas siltums recommends airing 2 to 4 times a day during the heating season, opening the window fully for 5 to 10 minutes, and turning off the thermostatic valve during that time.
