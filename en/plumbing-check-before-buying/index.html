Imanta’s blocks were mostly built in 1967–1975, so original pipes and valves may be older than their average service life. Some of the answers come from inspecting the flat, some from the building manager’s documents.

When it makes sense to bring in a plumber before the deal

  • The bathroom is original, with the old riser and cast-iron drains.
  • The bathroom has been freshly renovated and the pipes are behind tiles: then the question is what exactly was left there.
  • The flat has been rebuilt. In the Ministry of Economics study of the 464 series, in 13 buildings in Riga and Jelgava, places were found where rooms were combined in a rebuild.
  • You are buying a detached house in west Riga and do not know whether it is connected to the city sewer.

What a 1970s panel block means

The appendices to Cabinet Regulation No. 907 give the average service life of building parts. You can compare what you see in the flat against these figures. For 602-series blocks the normative service life is 60 years, and the first of them were built in 1971.

Part Average service life What it means if the part is original
Galvanised cold water pipe with threads 30 years Exceeded. The regulations add that galvanised pipes are not recommended.
Galvanised hot water pipe 15–20 years Exceeded several times over if the pipe has not been replaced.
Drains with cast-iron fittings 45 years Close to the limit or past it.
Drains with plastic fittings 70 years If cast iron has already been replaced with plastic, this part can last long.
Bathroom taps, ceramic basins and toilets 30 years Originals are past it.
Brass and cast-iron valves 40 years Originals are past it; check whether they close.

Service lives: appendices to Cabinet Regulation No. 907 of 28.09.2010; checked 17.11.2026.

In the flat and at the building manager

Where What to look at Why
Risers in the bathroom or toilet Material, transitions to the neighbouring sections, damp at the joints The riser is common property; replacing it is a decision for the building, not only for you.
Water meters Whether the connection point has a seal Under Cabinet Regulation No. 1013, if a seal is damaged or removed, the flat is excluded from the usual sharing of the water difference.
Angle valves and hoses Whether the valves close, whether the braiding has broken wires The Australian company Vero advises replacing braided metal hoses before they are five years old.
The wall by the bathroom unit and the panel joints Damp patches, swollen finish The 467A-series study advises checking the units if signs of damp are visible at the panel joints.
Building manager Results of the last building inspection Since 30.07.2026 a building’s water supply and drains in shared areas are inspected once a year.

How the check goes

  1. Before the viewing In the “Reģistri” (registers) section of BIS, in the register of residential building managers, find out who manages the building and ask for the results of the last inspection. The “Noderīgi” (useful) section has studies on the weak points of a particular series.
  2. Water Open the taps in the kitchen and bathroom and look at the stream and the colour of the water, then close each angle valve and the flat’s shut-off valve to see whether they hold.
  3. Drains Run water in the sink, bath and toilet, listen and watch whether it drains away, and inspect the accessible pipes up to the riser.
  4. Risers and meters Inspect the riser material and transitions, the meter seals and traces of damp in the boxing and on the ceiling.
  5. Documents If the bathroom has been moved, ask the seller for the current cadastral survey file and compare it with what is there. Only an application to the Building Board gives full clarity on whether the rebuild is lawful.

Plumbing check before buying a house

Rīgas ūdens reminded the owners of houses in Imantas līnijas: decentralised sewage systems had to comply with the requirements by 31.12.2021, but if there was already a city sewer in the street by the property, the house had to be connected by 31.12.2023. When buying a detached house, ask the seller whether the house is connected and, if not, what system there is in the yard.

Under the Law on Water Management Services, the water supply and sewerage beyond the boundary of responsibility belong to the house owner. So include in the inspection the incoming supply, the meter assembly and the outlet to the chamber too.

What the cost of the inspection depends on

The price page has no separate price for a check. On the call you go over what to inspect, and the plumber names the price. The rows “Plumber visit with fault finding” (from €40) and “Hourly rate for small jobs” (from €25/h) can be used. The price changes with the size of the flat or house, how many places have to be opened and whether the yard outlet has to be inspected too. Before the inspection begins, the call makes clear whether you pay anything for travel on top.

What residents ask

Can an inspection show pipes behind new tiles?

No, pipes behind the finish are not visible. You can check what comes out (valves, transitions, the riser in the hatch) and ask the seller what was replaced in the renovation and when.

The meter has no seal. Is that the buyer's problem?

Under Cabinet Regulation No. 1013, the connection point is sealed by the building manager or by whoever installed the meter. Without a seal the flat may be excluded from the usual sharing of the water difference, so it is better to settle this before the deal.

How do I find out who manages the building where I am buying a flat?

The “Reģistri” (registers) section of BIS has the register of residential building managers. There you can find the manager by address and ask about the risers and the last inspection.
