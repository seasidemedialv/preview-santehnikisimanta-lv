Shower enclosure installation - a low trap or a tray on legs

Shower enclosure installation in Imanta most often takes place where the old bath was, and the first choice is a tray drain kit.

If there is little room under the tray, a low trap suits, for example the Viega Tempoplex with a 60 mm build height, and if there is more, a tray on legs. Installation costs from €125 per set.

An enclosure in a concrete block, by a gypsum concrete wall and on a wooden floor

An enclosure in the bathroom/WC unit of a 602-series block

The Ministry of Economics study on the 602 series says that in these buildings the sanitary cabins are three-dimensional, of monolithic reinforced concrete and made in the factory as one module. The floor under the old bath is therefore part of the concrete block. The tray drain in such a room is usually solved above this floor: the tray is raised, and a low trap reduces the rise needed.

A tray by the gypsum concrete partition of a 464-series block

In the grey five-storey 464-series blocks the partitions are gypsum concrete. The Kudos instructions require the joint between the tray and the wall to be filled with silicone up to the top edge of the tray before the tiles or other wall finish, because later this spot is under it.

A tray on legs in a private house by Kooperatīva iela

On both sides of Kooperatīva iela in Imanta the private-house district took shape in the late 1940s and in the 1950s. If the bathroom floor of such a house is on wooden joists, the instructions of the Showers To You lifting kit require extra cross-timbers to be placed between the joists where needed. The tray must rest fully on every leg, otherwise it can crack.

The tray drains water before the door goes on

In the Kudos instructions the tray drain is checked once more after installation. If the water does not drain, first check that the tray is level. This manufacturer does not allow tiles, wall panels and the shower door to be fitted until the tray drains the water properly.

The glass parts of the enclosure have their own procedure. The instructions of the Ravak Pivot PSS door require the gap between the enclosure and the tray, and the vertical gap at the wall, to be sealed with silicone only from the outside, on a dry, firm and clean surface.

Shower enclosure repair: rollers, hinges and the outer seam

If the enclosure door slides heavily, look at the mechanism first. Omnires recommends oiling the hinges, joints, guides and rollers at least once a year with technical or silicone lubricant. A loosened outer seam is redone as at installation: from the outside, on a dry and clean surface.

What changes the amount: a drain outlet in a new place

Shower enclosure installation costs from €125 per set: assembling the enclosure, connecting the tray to the drain and the water supply, sealing the seams. What changes the amount most is the drain outlet. If the enclosure stands where the bath was, the tray is connected to the existing branch. If it moves to another corner, a new outlet is needed.

For example: in a Zolitūde 119-series flat the enclosure goes against the opposite wall, where there is no drain. Shower enclosure installation from €125 and a new water or drain outlet from €55 together give at least €180. That is labour only, materials extra. If there is a travel charge, you arrange it on the call before the enclosure installation starts.

What residents ask

How far apart may the tray legs be placed?

For a Kudos Easy-Plumb tray the distance between legs must not exceed 1000 mm, and all corners must be supported. For another manufacturer the figure can differ; check it in the instructions of the particular tray.

Can a Viega 60 mm trap be cleaned from above?

Yes. With the Viega Tempoplex kit the trap part can be lifted out upwards, so the tray does not have to be removed. The manufacturer also offers an extra hair strainer that catches hair before the trap.

What may be used to clean Ravak enclosure glass and profiles?

Ravak recommends cleaning painted parts, glass and plastic only with a soft cloth and an ordinary household cleaner. Abrasive pastes, solvents and acetone must not be used.
