Toilet installation in Imanta, Riga - connection and flush

Toilet installation in Imanta comes down to two questions: how to join the new toilet’s outlet to what lies behind the old toilet, and how many litres to flush.

TECE states that a 4.5-litre economy flush can be used in new buildings with pipes adapted to it, but existing pipe systems often need more, up to 9 litres. Replacing a toilet costs from €50 each.

Three toilets in Imanta and nearby

A floor-standing toilet at a cast-iron socket in a 464-series block

If there is still a cast-iron drain pipe behind the old toilet, the job is harder: in the T-Zh guide, a plumber names old-stock cast iron as the trickiest spot for fitting a toilet. The choice is between a 123 × 110 mm transition cuff, which goes into a socket that has been cleaned and treated with sealant, and a flexible WC connector. If the cast-iron pipe to the riser has cracked, waste pipe replacement comes first.

A new toilet on the 9th floor of a 602-series block

On an upper floor it is worth checking that the cistern fill valve works at the building’s pressure. For Gustavsberg Nautic toilets the manufacturer states a working pressure of 0.5–10 bar. In a pressure test with water against the valve up to 10 bar is allowed, and against a closed shut-off valve up to 16 bar.

A close-coupled toilet in a private house in Bieriņi leaks behind the toilet

If water appears between the cistern and the toilet, the T-Zh guide says the rubber seal most likely needs replacing, not the toilet. If it leaks right at the moment of flushing, behind the toilet, the culprit can be the corrugated connecting pipe: its material hardens over time and loses elasticity and tightness.

What to use to join a toilet to the drain

Connection When it is chosen Sizes and notes
Transition cuff 123 × 110 mm the cast-iron socket is intact and the toilet stands in the same place the socket is cleaned and treated with sealant (Lifehacker.ru guide)
McAlpine WC connector the outer diameter of the toilet outlet is 97–107 mm outlets of 110 mm (PVC and cast iron), 90 mm (cast iron) and 75 mm
McAlpine MACFIT the drain pipe is already in place and only the toilet has to be connected a soft rubber inlet with a screw clamp onto the toilet outlet
Corrugated connecting pipe already there at the old toilet T-Zh: hardens over time and starts to leak at the moment of flushing

Flexible WC connectors have no standard of their own in the UK, so McAlpine makes its own to the German standard DIN 1389:2000-05.

Dimensions to measure before buying

The type of outlet shows in the pipe behind the existing toilet: the T-Zh guide says that floor-standing toilets pushed up to the wall usually have a straight outlet, wall-hung ones a horizontal one. Next, compare the heights. For example, for the LAUFEN Kartell floor-standing toilet the outlet is 185 mm above the finished floor, the water inlet at 380 mm, and the S-trap outlet 95–120 mm from the wall; other models have other figures.

A floor-standing toilet is held down by screws. The Geberit instructions require a floor-standing toilet to be fixed mechanically and, before installation, the load-bearing capacity of the floor and whether the fixings suit the type of floor to be checked.

Installing a concealed toilet without chasing the wall

If you want to hide the cistern, there are three routes. The Geberit Monolith module stands on the finished floor and suits toilets whose water inlet is 32–36 cm above the floor. The TECEbox 820 mm is a low in-wall cistern for a floor-standing toilet, also under a window, and its service tunnel adjusts to a finish of 20 to 90 mm. For the Geberit Duofix 111.350.00.6 frame, the height of a wall-hung toilet is set from 41 to 46 cm.

With the GROHE Rapid SL frame, the instructions require the toilet to be installed only after tiling and the flush pipe at the toilet to be fitted without grease or oil. If an in-wall cistern is already there and just does not work, see concealed cistern repair.

A bidet or hygiene shower in a narrow toilet room

A bidet needs a cold and hot water supply, its own drain connection and free space beside the toilet. A hygiene shower needs only a water supply. The supply is priced from the “Water supply connection point” line, from €35, and a drain outlet in a new place from the “New water or drain outlet” line, from €55.

What replacing a toilet costs

Replacing a toilet from €50 includes removing the floor-standing toilet and fitting the new one with the drain and water connections. The sum changes most with the condition of the cast-iron socket behind the old toilet. If the pipe to the riser has to be replaced, the “Replacing a drain pipe in a flat” line is added, from €30 per connection point. Any travel charge, if there is one, you discuss on the call before the old toilet is removed. Labour only, materials extra.

What residents ask

What silicone suits under the base of a floor-standing toilet?

The Gustavsberg Nautic instructions specify sanitary silicone meeting class EN 15651-3 XS1 and a Ø 110 mm drain connection. The Lifehacker.ru guide advises applying silicone in a snake line along the perimeter, 2–3 cm from the edge.

The water inlet in my old toilet room is low. Does the Geberit Monolith suit?

The Geberit Monolith module is intended for toilets whose water inlet is 32–36 cm above the floor (EN 33). If the inlet is at another height, check it before buying or choose another solution.

The new toilet flushes weakly. Can the flush be increased?

If the cistern allows it, yes. TECE states that existing pipe systems often need a larger flush than 4.5 litres, and its cisterns can be set up to 9 litres. The setting is changed in the cistern, not in the toilet.
