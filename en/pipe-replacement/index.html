Choosing the new pipe is easier than deciding what to do with what stays next to it: steel after copper, a brass fitting and a shut-off valve on a branch. That is why every transition between the old and the new pipe is planned separately.

Where old pipework gives out in Imanta buildings

A thread under the sink in a 464-series bathroom

Baunetz Wissen explains that galvanising does not protect a pipe from rust at the cut points, so in old pipework the threads are the weakest spot. Galvanised steel is joined only with threads and galvanised malleable-iron fittings: the Gablingen water supply leaflet (after EN 806-2) says that welding, soldering or bending on site damages the zinc layer. A rusted thread is therefore not patched, but the section up to the next joint is replaced.

A shut-off valve on a branch in a 602-series kitchen

The HANSA-FLEX ball valve instructions say that such a valve is kept fully open or fully closed; half open it damages the ball seals, and the valve starts to leak or sticks. If a tap has stood half open for years, it may not close in an emergency. When a new brass valve is screwed on, Milwaukee Valve advises holding the end of the valve that is being connected with a smooth-jaw wrench, otherwise the inside of the valve can be deformed. To replace this valve, the riser is shut off by the building manager’s service.

An incoming supply in a private house in the Imanta lines

In phase 6 Rīgas ūdens built a water main in the 1st–3rd Imanta lines, and for houses that connect to it the pipe from the ownership boundary to the house is the owner’s responsibility. If it is laid in PP-R in an unheated house or in autumn, it is worth knowing the KAN-therm guidance. Below +5 °C the heating time for welding is extended by 50 %, and below 0 °C welding should not be done.

What happens where two materials meet

Joint What happens to it What to take into account
Galvanised steel downstream of copper Baunetz Wissen: steel corrodes from water that contains copper Do not create this order; do not leave a galvanised pipe after copper parts
Steel directly against copper Galvanic corrosion, which grows with the water temperature A dielectric coupling, for example Boshart DU-NL with a nylon insulator, about 17 bar and 82 °C
A brass fitting on a crooked pipe Gablingen leaflet: a stressed fitting can develop stress corrosion cracking The pipe must be straight against the fitting, without force
Hot water pipe EN 806-2: galvanised materials and those at risk of dezincification are not recommended for hot water If only part is replaced, the hot water side is chosen first
New PP-R against existing PP-R KAN-therm: only parts from the same manufacturer are welded together You need to know which manufacturer the existing pipe is from

A multilayer pipe is simpler in this respect. Geberit states that Mepla 16 and 20 mm pipes can be bent by hand and with a bending tool up to 50 mm, so there are fewer joints where something can go wrong.

How many turns for a thread

A threaded metal joint leaks if it is too loose and cracks if it is overtightened. For its joints up to 1 inch Boshart advises turning another 1.5–3 full turns after tightening by hand, and 3.5–6 threads must be screwed in. If there are fewer threads, the joint is too loose.

For the dielectric coupling the manufacturer adds one more thing: its seal expands and contracts with the water temperature, so the joint may start to leak over time and has to be checked regularly. It is not walled in, but left accessible.

Connection points set the sum more than metres do

In a flat the pipe metres are the cheap part: water pipe laying is from €10/m, while each water supply connection point is from €35. That is why a short run with three fixtures usually costs more than a long run to one. The price per metre is for the pipe on top of the finish; chasing and finishing are a separate job.

For example: in a 464-series flat only the hot water pipe is replaced, from the shut-off valve to the bath and sink mixer taps, about 3 m, while the cold steel stays. Pipe laying at least €30, two connection points at least €70, together at least €100, labour only, materials extra.

You go over the job and the price on the call, before the plumber starts. Whether there is a separate charge for travel, the plumber says on that same call.

What residents ask

Can a dielectric coupling be tiled over in a boxed-in section?

Better not. Boshart states that the seal expands and contracts with the water temperature, the joint may start to leak, and it has to be checked regularly. A boxed-in section needs a hatch through which it can be seen.

How often does a 20 mm PP-R pipe need a fixing to the wall?

For a plain 20 mm PP-R pipe KAN-therm gives no less often than every 0.60 m for cold water and 0.50 m at 80 °C. A pipe with a fibreglass layer needs one every 0.90 m, and for vertical sections the distance may be increased by 30 %.

The old pipework is already PP-R, but the manufacturer is unknown. Can the new section be welded on?

KAN-therm states that only parts from the same manufacturer may be welded together. If the manufacturer cannot be identified from the marking on the pipe, the new section needs a different kind of joint, which the plumber chooses on site.
