A plumber in Šampēteris works in a neighbourhood where homes grew up next to factories for centuries: a vinegar manufactory appeared here in 1803, and a wool manufactory operated from 1895 to 1914.

Now wooden houses and multi-storey blocks stand between them. The apartment block at Kalnciema iela 106, which our map reading shows in Šampēteris, was renovated in 2023 with ALTUM support. Before replacing flat pipes in such a building, it is worth knowing what has already been renovated.

Residential blocks between factory fences

The street grid here is old. By 1891 the lands of the former manor were divided into more than 80 plots, and the new streets received the names of Courland manors: Bārtas, Zalves, Volguntes. Factories operated next to the plots, and in place of the “Pihlau” chemical plant there is now a metal and machinery plant. From Imanta it is about 2.5 km to here (our map reading).

Ponds remain from the factories. The area of the Zasulauks Wool Manufactory designed by Wilhelm Bockslaff, with its pond, is partly abandoned, and at the end of Vecaines iela you can still see the pond used by the brewery founded by F. Schindler in 1926 at Šampēteris manor. Near such a spot the ground of a house can be wetter or filled, so a yard pipe needs a firm bed and the incoming supply through the basement wall needs good sealing.

The wooden residential building of Šampēteris manor, which held flats after the war, was demolished in 1998. On such cleared plots, old pipes or a well can remain in the ground. If a small house on an old plot has no drawing showing where the incoming supply and the outlet run, the route is established on site before digging. It starts from the meter location and the point where the pipe passes through the foundations.

Kalnciema iela 106 after renovation

Our map reading: OpenStreetMap shows the address Kalnciema iela 106 in Šampēteris. In the Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks list this apartment block was renovated with support from the ALTUM energy efficiency programme and put into service on 18.10.2023. The total cost was €1,102,045.

If after the building’s renovation you also plan to sort out the bathroom, first ask the building manager what exactly was changed in the common systems and where in your flat the building’s part begins. Article 9 of the Law on the Management of Residential Buildings makes the building’s owner responsible for its management, and in an apartment block that means all the flat owners together.

In the flat you then replace the pipes from the riser branch to the taps and the drain to the riser. A plastic drain is joined to an old cast iron end with a special transition piece. For a 110 mm transition, for example, the manufacturer Pipelife gives a sealing zone that suits a cast iron end with an outside diameter of 110–116 mm. A drain fall that changes on a dune ridge

A yard drain to the street network or inspection chamber needs an even fall. Near Šampētera iela and Candera iela the less disturbed Zasulauks dunes have survived, but elsewhere they were dug off and levelled for construction, so the pipe route can look quite different at two neighbouring houses.

Sediment tends to build up where the fall suddenly lessens, for example at a bend or chamber. The neighbourhood has almost no surface water, only two small ponds in the east by Šampētera iela, 0.5 ha in total. If the basement is damp, it is worth checking the pipes and the yard drain first.

Such a drain is cleaned with a snake or jetted with high-pressure water. The plastic pipe manufacturers' association TEPPFA recommends inspecting the pipe with a video camera after the blockage has been flushed out, to find the cause, for example a crack or a collapse.

What residents ask

Our building on Kalnciema iela has been renovated. Can we replace the pipes in the flat up to the riser?

You can replace your own part in the flat, but the riser belongs to all the flat owners together. Before the work, ask the building manager where that boundary lies in your building and whether the riser will need to be shut off for a time.

Nobody knows where the water supply runs to a wooden house on an old plot. Where do we start?

With the meter location and the point where the pipe enters the house through the foundations. From there the route can be followed to the inspection chamber or the street. Once the route is known, on the call you go over the amount of digging, the job and the price.

A yard drain runs down from a dune ridge and blocks at the same place. Why?

Often it is a point where the fall or direction changes: a bend or a chamber. A camera inspection after jetting shows whether jetting now and then is enough or this section has to be re-laid.

Rīgas ūdens flushed the mains in Šampēteris. Can that affect a toilet cistern?

Planned flushing in this neighbourhood took place in 2020. Rīgas ūdens explains that during flushing the water can briefly turn cloudy. If the cistern fills slowly, first check the filter of the fill valve; Geberit, for example, states that its valve filter can be replaced in a few steps. Replacing a flushing mechanism is from €35 per job.
