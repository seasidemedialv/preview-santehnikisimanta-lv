A hundred years in Riga with its own well and tank

Beberbeķi was included in the territory of the city of Riga on 24 October 1922, but a street water main and sewer appeared here only after 2021. For almost a century the houses lived with their own well and their own tank, so in an older house you can meet pipes from several periods: some laid at the well, some later, some after connection to the mains.

On 17 October 2014 the Riga Building Board announced a public consultation on changing the addresses of part of Beberbeķi. For example, Beberbeķu 7. līnija 15 was to get the address Krotes iela 22, and Beberbeķu iela 38 k-1 the address Čuguna iela 44. The old well, tank or water contract papers may show the old address, so when you compare them with newer documents, first check that they are about the same house.

The name of the neighbourhood comes from the small river Neriņa, once called Bebra, which flowed through the bog behind the row of dunes. If the house stands in the lowest part towards Mūkupurvs, the fall of the yard pipe to the street is checked before digging, not after. It is about 3.6 km from Imanta to Beberbeķi (read from our map).

A house, a workshop, a yard without an inspection chamber

Type of building What it means for plumbing
A detached house with a garden, where a tree grows by the pipe route If a blockage keeps returning in the same section by the tree, the cause shows only in a camera inspection: roots in a joint, a collapse or a section with no fall.
A workshop or commercial building on a mixed-use plot Mixed-use areas take up 27% or 32.6 ha of Beberbeķi. Such a building has its own toilets and sinks, and the job may be in a small company’s premises, not only in a house.
A house with no inspection chamber in the yard The yard pipe is then reached from the house side: through the cleanout in the outlet at the foundations or in the house. If there is none, it is worth installing one when a section of pipe is being dug up anyway.

Roots in the pipe: jet, cut or replace a section

TEPPFA states that mechanical root cutters carry a high risk of damaging the pipe and are only a temporary solution, because the roots grow back. In the long term, replacing the section or removing the tree helps. In its jetting machines, the manufacturer ROTHENBERGER provides a separate Root Ranger nozzle for roots, used to clear the roots after the penetrating nozzle has gone through the blockage.

If the yard pipe is plastic, the limit is known: in the guidelines for jetting plastic pipes (GAP), the maximum recommended pressure for PVC-U, PE and PP pipes in good condition is 120 bar. TEPPFA mentions that 60 bar is usually enough for soft deposits, while thicker layers may need 80–120 bar.

A camera inspection has its own standard: EN 13508-2 sets a coding system for describing the condition of drains and chambers outside buildings, starting from the point where waste water leaves the building. It can decide whether drain jetting is enough or a section must be replaced.

  1. Find the cleanout For a yard pipe it is reached from the outlet of the house or from an inspection chamber in the yard.
  2. Clear or jet it A soft blockage is cleared with a drain snake; grease, sand and roots are jetted out with high-pressure water.
  3. Inspect with a camera You see where the pipe has collapsed, where it has no fall and where roots are growing in.

For example: a yard pipe by an apple tree blocks again and again. Two hours of jetting at the rate from €60 per hour is at least €120 for the work. The price of a camera inspection depends on the length of the pipe and access, on whether the pipe must be jetted before the inspection, and on whether a recording is needed. You learn on the call, before the plumber sets out for Beberbeķi, whether you pay extra for travel. If the blockage is in the house’s internal drains, see drain unblocking.

What residents ask

The yard pipe is plastic. What pressure can it be jetted at?

In the guidelines for jetting plastic pipes (GAP), the maximum recommended pressure for PVC-U, PE and PP pipes in good condition is 120 bar; if there is a crack or a collapse in the pipe, you see it first on camera. Jetting with high-pressure water costs from €60 per hour (at least 1 h).

A tree grows by the pipe. Can roots block a yard drain?

They can. In the TEPPFA survey, tree roots caused 14% of blockages. If the pipe blocks repeatedly, it is jetted and inspected with a camera to see whether roots have grown into a joint, and then you decide about replacing the section.

There is no inspection chamber in the yard. How is the pipe to the street cleared then?

From the house side, through the cleanout in the outlet. The snake or the jetting hose is fed in there and driven towards the street. Yard drain cleaning costs from €50 per call-out (1st hour), jetting with high-pressure water from €60 per hour.

The old well papers give a Beberbeķi line address, but now our street is different. Is it the same house?

Possibly. In 2014 the Building Board proposed changing the address of some Beberbeķi houses, for example Beberbeķu 7. līnija 15 was to become Krotes iela 22. In the form, write the current address, and if the papers show the old one, mention that too.
