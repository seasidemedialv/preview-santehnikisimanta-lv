Pedestrian streets and half-enclosed courtyards

The multi-storey part of Zolitūde is planned around pedestrian streets leading to the Imanta and Zolitūde stations. The blocks are arranged in groups around wide, half-enclosed courtyards, while on Zolitūdes, Ruses, Paula Lejiņa and Aleksandra Bieziņa streets they stand in a row along the street as perimeter development.

One practical thing follows for plumbing work: you cannot always drive right up to the stairwell. An old bath, a toilet unit or a long pipe section is carried across the courtyard. So it helps to state in the form which street the nearest entrance is from, which floor the flat is on and whether the building has a lift.

Only Mazā Zolitūdes iela, across the railway, connects Zolitūde with Imanta in the street network; all other routes go along Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve and Lielirbes iela. It is about 1.8 km from Imanta to Zolitūde (read from our map).

A shower by the window, a toilet behind a new wall

The 119 series was designed entirely in Latvia. Its flats were 15% larger than the Soviet standard, and the series is known for letting you rebuild a flat without fear of taking down partition walls. That is why a renovation plan often includes the idea of joining the bathroom to the toilet or moving the shower to the corner of the former hallway.

A wall can be moved, a soil stack cannot. It stays in its shaft, and every new outlet needs an even fall towards it. For example, the Swedish industry rules (Säker Vatten) say that with a slab 200 mm thick the riser should be no more than 3.5 m from the toilet outlet and 5 m from other outlets. This is not a norm in Latvia, but it shows well how far a toilet can realistically be moved.

An explanation from the Riga Building Board (LV portal, 2016) says that whether a simplified procedure is enough for a flat rebuild also depends on whether plumbing units are moved. Rebuilding shared building services needs a building design and a building permit. If a bathroom is joined to a room, the layout is drawn for the whole flat, not only for the rebuilt part.

Riga Building Board explanation on moving plumbing units in a flat, LV portal, checked 17.11.2026.

For example: a shower is moved next to the window and needs a new water outlet and a new drain outlet. At the “New water or drain outlet” price (from €55 per outlet) that is at least €110 for the work, materials extra. If the old drain pipe from the sink to the riser has to be replaced, “Replacing a drain pipe in a flat” is added, from €30 per connection point. Whether travel has its own charge, the plumber tells you on the call when you go over the job.

How outlets are made before tiling is described on the moving plumbing outlets page, and replacing the old drain as far as the riser on the waste pipe replacement page.

After the flushing of the water mains

Rīgas ūdens flushed the water mains in Zolitūde as planned in 2020, in the same round as Imanta, Dzirciems, Zasulauks and Šampēteris. The company explains that salts and minerals in the water settle in the pipes over time and can come loose when the pressure changes. During flushing, short interruptions and cloudy water are possible, but the water is fit to use.

In a flat this shows up in small places. If a tap runs weaker after the water was shut off, first unscrew the aerator at the end of the tap and rinse it. The washing machine has its own filter at the end of the supply hose. If the flow is still weak after that, or the tap drips, the job is mixer tap replacement or a check of the supply.

Replacing risers is a different matter. Under Section 13 of the Law on Residential Property, a shared riser is paid for by all the flat owners, each their own share, as the owners' association decides. If your rebuild affects a riser, ask the building manager first.

What residents ask

Can a bathroom be joined to the toilet in a 119-series flat?

The series is known for allowing partition walls to be taken down. On the plumbing side, you have to check that the toilet and shower in the new place reach the riser with a fall. You also have to find out from the Riga Building Board what procedure is needed, because moving plumbing units affects it.

The flat is in a courtyard. What should I put in the form?

The street and building number, the stairwell, the floor and which side of the courtyard the nearest entrance is on. If an old bath has to be carried out or long pipes carried in, mention it in the description, so that you can go over the job and the price on the call.

After the water was shut off, cloudy water comes from the tap. Is that a fault?

Rīgas ūdens explains that after flushing and pressure changes the water can be briefly cloudy. Let it run and rinse the tap aerator. If the tap then drips or runs weakly, describe it in the form.

The rebuild affects a riser. Who pays for it?

A riser is usually common property, and the flat owners pay for its replacement in proportion to their shares, as the association decides, unless the building’s documents say otherwise. The building manager will tell you how it is in your building.
