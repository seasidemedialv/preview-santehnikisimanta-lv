The manufacturer Pipelife states that its 110 mm transition seal suits cast iron of 110–116 mm. In the same bathroom the branch angle and the distance to the riser are measured too, because they decide where the toilet and the shower can stand.

A 45° branch and the toilet straight onto the riser

When you rebuild a bathroom, decide early where each fixture will connect to the drain. The Swedish industry rules Säker Vatten give a clear order here. The toilet outlet may run straight to the riser, while the pipes from the sink, bath, shower and washing machine join lower down on a branch at an angle of no more than 45°. This makes it harder for waste to flow back from the toilet.

The same rules warn that renovations often turn up an old arrangement: a fixture connected straight down into a horizontal pipe in the floor slab. So that every connection has an angle of no more than 45°, such a pipe has to be moved, and this is planned before anyone cuts or drills the concrete.

The second measure is distance. With a slab 200 mm thick, Säker Vatten says the riser should be no more than 3.5 m from the toilet outlet and 5 m from other outlets, and in practice the permitted length can be even shorter. If you want to move the toilet to the opposite wall, measure this distance before you choose tiles and furniture.

For a pipe laid in a chase and plastered over, the manufacturer Pipelife recommends wrapping it in insulation, for example 4 mm PE pipe insulation, so that it does not touch the wall and carries less noise. The chase must let the pipe lie in it without tension.

A flat owner does not replace the riser alone: a decision by the flat owners or the building manager’s consent is usually needed, in line with the building’s decisions, so ask the building manager. The job is described on the riser replacement page, and new outlets on the moving plumbing outlets page.

From the Zunds bank to Dzegužkalns

The eastern edge of Dzirciems, by the Zunds canal, is at sea level, and the Zunds there is 50 to 200 m wide and 3 to 7 m deep. In the north of the neighbourhood rises Dzegužkalns: 28 m above sea level, the highest natural point of land in Riga. The Riga Stradiņš University complex at Dzirciema iela 16 (architect V. Čikste, 1987) is one of the largest university complexes in Latvia, and until the early 20th century its site was the marshy meadows of the Zasu manor.

One practical thing from this picture helps with bathroom work. In April 2022 Riga City Council, together with Rīgas ūdens, opened a free drinking-water tap in Dzegužkalns park. If the water in your building is shut off on the day of the rebuild, this is a place in the neighbourhood to fetch drinking water. From Imanta to Dzirciems it is about 3.0 km (read from our map).

A toilet in a former factory building

Along Daugavgrīvas iela stand buildings with a long industrial history. In 1910 the Mantel and Salat machine factory at Daugavgrīvas iela 31 was extended with an iron and metal foundry. At Daugavgrīvas iela 63/65, the aniline dye factory “Leopold Cassella & Co”, founded in 1898, operated until the mid-1930s, and since 1951 the Riga paint and varnish factory has stood there. At Daugavgrīvas iela 43 the E. Veiss piano factory worked in the 1920s.

Buildings like these were built for production, not for living, so a new toilet or kitchen sink in an office or workshop can end up far from the existing drain line. In Pipelife’s instructions (based on the Austrian standard ÖNORM B 2501), a single connecting pipe longer than 4 m or with more than 3 bends needs separate ventilation. So the route is measured and the bends are counted before the walls are closed.

If the new plastic pipe has to be joined to an old cast-iron pipe, a special transition piece is used. Pipelife states that the 110 mm transition seal zone suits a cast-iron end with an outer diameter of 110–116 mm, so the old end is measured first. If nobody knows where the existing pipes run, the first visit finds them: a plumber’s visit with fault finding costs from €40.

What residents ask

We want to connect the shower drain straight to the riser, like the toilet. Is that allowed?

Under the Swedish industry rules Säker Vatten, only the toilet outlet runs straight to the riser, while the shower, bath and sink pipes join lower down on a branch at an angle of no more than 45°. The principle is simple: a branch makes it harder for waste to flow back from the toilet into the shower drain.

The water in the building will be shut off on the day of the rebuild. Where can I get drinking water in Dzirciems?

In Dzegužkalns park there is a free city drinking-water tap, opened by Riga City Council and Rīgas ūdens in April 2022. Before you go, check that it is working that day.

In an office in a former factory the toilet will be 6 m from the drain. Can it be built?

It can, but under the manufacturer Pipelife’s instructions a connecting pipe longer than 4 m or with more than 3 bends needs separate ventilation. This is planned before the walls are finished. In the form, write the distance and whether the pipe will run through the floor or the wall; you go over the job and the price on the call.
