A list for one visit

A plumber by the hour suits a trip like this. The hourly rate for small jobs is from €25/h, and usually at least the first hour is paid. If what exactly is broken first has to be found, a plumber visit with fault finding is charged, from €40.

In a house with its own water heater the list often includes water heater descaling. For its PSH Classic model the manufacturer Stiebel Eltron advises removing loose limescale deposits from the tank during servicing, descaling the flange only after it has been removed, and never treating the tank surface and the protective anode with descalers. Water heater descaling costs from €75: the tank is drained, the limescale is removed, and the anode and the heating element are checked.

For example: the water heater has to be descaled, the inlet valve in the toilet cistern replaced, and a mixer tap in the kitchen changed. “Water heater descaling” from €75, “Flush mechanism replacement” from €35 and “Mixer tap replacement” from €25 together come to at least €135 for the work, materials extra. The plumber states on the call, before starting, whether reaching Kleisti costs anything extra.

Rain from the roof is not for the drain

On 26 August 2026 Riga City Council, with binding regulations No. RD-26-55-sn, designated a cemetery area next to the Lāčupe cemeteries. The land unit is at 30 Baltegļu iela in Kleisti, in place of the existing detached house development. The regulations require separate sewerage: domestic wastewater is kept apart from rainwater, and rainwater from roofs is infiltrated into the ground or collected in tanks.

These are regulations for one land unit, not for your house, but the principle works in a detached house too. Roof downpipes and yard gullies are not connected to the house sewer or the holding tank, because rainwater loads them exactly when it rains the most. An infiltration spot or a tank of your own keeps the rainwater separate.

The same regulations require burial places to have a groundwater depth of at least 2.5 m, and a columbarium at least 2 m from the ground surface, and they provide for putting the drainage system in order. If your house has a basement or a technical pit, note it in the form, because then the place where the pipes pass through the foundation is inspected too.

Outdoor tap, stable and frost

For watering the cemetery, the regulations for 30 Baltegļu iela provide free taps so that from any point of the cemetery none is farther than 50 m away. At 4 Rātsupītes iela there is a sports base with horse stables and riding arenas: the Kleisti modern pentathlon and equestrian sports centre opened in 1980, and since 29 July 2025 it has been managed by the Latvian National Sports Centre.

In a detached house the outdoor tap and the supply pipe to a shed or garage are not heated, so they freeze first in winter. If such a supply pipe has its own shut-off valve in the house, close it before the frost and leave the outdoor tap open so no water stays in the pipe. If there is no such valve, one can be fitted: “Water supply connection point” costs from €35 per point, and a new pipe to the shed, “Water pipe laying”, from €10/m.

What residents ask

Can the water heater tank be rinsed with a descaler?

The manufacturer Stiebel Eltron forbids it for its PSH Classic model: the tank surface and the protective anode are not treated with descalers. Loose deposits are removed, and the flange is descaled only after it has been removed. For other manufacturers, see the instructions for your own water heater.

There is no sewer in the street, and the house has a holding tank. Can the roof drainage go into it?

Better not. Rainwater fills the tank right when it rains, and then it has to be emptied more often. The regulations for 30 Baltegļu iela in Kleisti show the city’s approach: rainwater from roofs is infiltrated into the ground or collected in separate tanks.

There is a tap in the garage in Kleisti, but no separate shut-off valve in the house. What should I do before winter?

Fit a shut-off valve on the garage supply pipe on the warm side of the house, so that in autumn the pipe can be closed off and drained. A water supply connection point costs from €35 per point. Write it in the form together with the other jobs, so that it can be done in one visit.
