If a workshop or office is fitted into a former workshop hall, the new toilet can be far from the drain connection. In the old wooden houses the attention goes to the fixings of the cast iron drain, because Wavin explains that structure-borne noise travels further through brackets and walls.

Manufactories, the station and the depot

Zasulauks was a place of production long before apartment blocks. In 1810 a woollen cloth manufactory opened here, and in 1833 a steam engine was installed as the power source on the site of the earlier mill. At the start of the 20th century about 40 enterprises used Zasulauks station for freight. The station building was built in 1912, its premises have a total area of over 1300 m², and in 1950 a motor-train depot was founded in Zasulauks.

A block of 20th-century industrial buildings still stands at the junction of Kuldīgas iela and Daugavgrīvas iela. If such a building now holds a workshop or office, the toilet and kitchen sink there were fitted later than the walls were built, and the route to the drain connection can be long. Such work starts with an inspection of the existing route. From Imanta to Zasulauks it is about 2.6 km (by the map).

Transition from cast iron to plastic

A cast iron drain can be replaced in sections, from the fixture to the common vertical pipe. Where the new plastic pipe meets the old cast iron, a transition piece is needed. For its RAUPIANO PLUS system the manufacturer REHAU makes this with an EPDM rubber sleeve and stainless steel clamps tightened to 3 Nm, and such a joint withstands 1 bar.

In a wooden house the second question is noise. Wavin explains that airborne noise is heard in the room the pipe runs through, while structure-borne noise travels through brackets and walls. So the fixing is as important as the pipe: Pipelife states that one of its acoustic clamps reduces structure-borne noise by 5 dB(A) and a double clamp by 10 dB(A), compared with a standard metal clamp with rubber.

The pipe wall matters too. Wavin compares the two. An ordinary thin-wall plastic pipe has a density of 0.9 and a wall of 2 mm, while mineral-filled AS+ has a density of 1.9 and a wall of 5.3 mm. The heavier and thicker the pipe, the more sound it absorbs. The job description is on the page drain pipe replacement.

After water main works on Jūrmalas gatve and Tapešu iela

In August 2025 Rīgas ūdens signed a contract for water main renewal by the bursting method in the Imanta and Zasulauks neighbourhoods, and one of the parts of the procurement was Jūrmalas gatve and Tapešu iela. Tapešu iela itself took its name from the wallpaper factory that operated here from 1884.

Rīgas ūdens explains that salts and minerals deposit over time on the walls of main pipes and can come loose with pressure changes. After a street main is replaced they can reach the taps in the house. The company carried out planned flushing of the Zasulauks mains in 2020.

Particles stop first in strainers and filters. In SCHELL angle valves with a filter, the inner filter can be removed with a coin or screwdriver and rinsed under running water. If the whole house is weak, not just one tap, Unitywater points out that a shut-off valve at the meter that is not fully open also reduces pressure. For cleaning several filters and strainers, a plumber by the hour suits: hourly rate for small jobs from €25/h.

What residents ask

A house by the Botanical Garden, and the yard drain blocks every few months. Are roots to blame?

It is possible. In a TEPPFA survey, drain jetting contractors in the United Kingdom named tree roots as the cause of a blockage in 14% of cases. Yard drain cleaning is from €50 per call-out (1st hour). If the blockage recurs, the pipe can be inspected with a camera; its price depends on the pipe length and access and on whether the pipe has to be jetted before the inspection.

After the water main works on Tapešu iela the tap is fully open but the flow is weak. What can I check myself?

First the strainer screwed into the end of the tap and, if there is an angle valve with a filter under the sink, that too. If all the taps in the house run weakly, check that the shut-off valve at the meter is fully open. If that does not help, describe in the form which taps have a weak flow.

We live behind the rails on the Zārdu iela side. What should we write in the form?

The part of Zasulauks between the Bolderāja and Jūrmala railway lines and Zārdu iela is cut off from the rest of the neighbourhood, so with the address add the street by which the house can be reached.
