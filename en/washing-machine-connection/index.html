Washing machine connection - pressure, tray and drain

Connecting a washing machine in Imanta starts with the water main pressure: when rebuilding the Jūrmalas gatve water main, Rīgas ūdens planned to separate high-pressure and low-pressure supply zones.

In the Swedish industry rules Säker Vatten, a machine must withstand a static pressure of at least 1 MPa (10 bar), otherwise a pressure reducing valve is fitted. Connection costs from €35 per machine.

Where the machine stands in Imanta and Zolitūde flats

A washing machine in a 602-series concrete bathroom unit

The sanitary cabins of the 602 series are monolithic reinforced-concrete modules made in a factory. Säker Vatten envisages a washing machine in a room with a waterproof floor and a floor drain. If there is no drain, the machine’s own protection matters a great deal: for example, in the Miele WWH 660 leaked water collects in the base tray, where a float closes the inlet valves.

A washing machine in the kitchen because there is no room in the bathroom

The Säker Vatten kitchen guidelines say that a third of water damage in flats happens in the kitchen, mainly from appliances connected to the water supply. These rules allow a washing machine in the kitchen only on a tested tray with a moisture sensor connected to a leak valve or an alarm, and with a shut-off valve whose handle is easy to reach. In a multi-storey building that tray also protects the neighbour’s ceiling below.

A machine in a 119-series flat in Zolitūde after a rebuild

The 119 series is known for allowing a flat to be rebuilt on a large scale and partition walls to be taken down, so the machine may get a new place. Säker Vatten warns that renovations often meet old solutions where a fixture is connected straight down into a horizontal pipe in the floor slab. Such a pipe is rerouted so that the connection is at no more than 45°, and this is planned before sawing or drilling concrete.

The supply hose: filters and 7000 kPa

The Miele WWH 660 has filters at the end of the supply hose and at the inlet, which protect the inlet valve, and they must not be removed. A replacement hose must be an original Miele hose tested at a pressure above 7000 kPa. So the replacement hose is taken from the manufacturer, not from the old machine.

The supply valve has its own requirement. According to the Miele PFD 104 SCVi instructions, the tap must be easy to reach, so that the water can be shut off when the machine is not in use.

Dishwasher connection: a loop at the height of the basket guide

The Miele PFD 104 SCVi has a non-return valve in the drain, and the hose is best connected to a separate outlet or a double-chamber trap. If the drain connection is lower than the lower basket’s roller guide with the door open, the hose is raised in a loop to that height. Otherwise, during a programme, a siphon effect can pull the water out of the machine.

The drain for this model must take at least 16 l/min. In the kitchen Säker Vatten advises leading the sink drain over the cupboard floor, so that a leak between the pipe and the trap is easy to notice. Behind the pipes towards the wall it advises at least 50 mm, between the pipes at least 60 mm.

What changes the cost: is there a valve and a drain where the machine goes

Connecting a washing machine or dishwasher costs from €35 per unit: connecting the supply valve and the drain, and a test with the first fill of water. One thing changes the cost: whether there is already a water supply and a drain where the machine goes. If not, a water connection point from €35 per point and a new water or drain outlet from €55 are added.

For example: a washing machine is moved from a 602-series bathroom to the kitchen, where there is neither a valve nor a drain. Connection from €35, a supply point from €35 and a drain outlet from €55 together give at least €125. Labour only, materials extra. What residents ask

How do I know whether a machine needs a pressure reducing valve?

Check the machine’s instructions for the static pressure it withstands. Sweden’s Säker Vatten requires appliances to withstand at least 1 MPa (10 bar). If the machine does not, a pressure reducing valve is fitted on the supply.

May the drain hose of a Miele dishwasher be shortened?

No. The drain hose of the Miele PFD 104 SCVi has a 22 mm inner diameter, and the manufacturer forbids shortening it. The supply hose with the water protection system is not shortened either, because it contains electrical parts.

What kind of tray must go under a dishwasher?

Säker Vatten requires the dishwasher and its connections to stand above a collecting tray with a moisture sensor that shuts off the water or sounds an alarm in case of a leak. If the sensor has a battery, it must be easy to replace.
