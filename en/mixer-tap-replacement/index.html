The tap drips after a cartridge change: pins and grooves

If the tap drips even with a new cartridge, check how it was fitted. Grohe stresses that the new cartridge’s seals must sit in the grooves and the base locating pins must go into the holes in the body, because a wrongly fitted cartridge may not seal.

In the Grohe Eurosmart Cosmopolitan tap the lever is held by a screw that is undone with a 3 mm hex key, while the cartridge nut needs a 32 mm spanner. For the supplies this manufacturer has its own order: cold water is connected on the right, hot on the left. The test pressure for this tap is 16 bar.

What residents ask

How do I know whether a cartridge can still be bought for an Oras tap?

Oras supplies spare parts for its taps for 10 years. For an older tap the parts may no longer exist, so a photo of the tap and, if known, the model name or the old cartridge’s size are useful in the form.

Why does the tap start spurting or dripping after the riser was shut off?

When the riser is filled again, particles from the pipes can reach the tap with the water. First rinse the angle valve filter, if there is one, and the tap’s strainer. If the tap drips even when closed, in Kohler’s explanation the cause is the cartridge or the check valve.

Can a temperature limiter be fitted to a Grohe tap?

For the Grohe Eurosmart Cosmopolitan basin tap the manufacturer states the option of fitting a temperature limiter to prevent scalding. The maximum hot water temperature for this tap is 80 °C.
