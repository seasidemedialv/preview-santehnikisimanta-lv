The valve under the sink and the valve in the basement

One boundary repeats in all four pieces of advice. The angle valve under the sink and the flat’s valve you can turn off yourself, but the riser is shut off in the basement, and that is usually the job of the building manager’s emergency service. A private house has no building manager: the main valve is at the incoming supply or the meter, and everything beyond the ownership boundary is the owner’s responsibility.

Where your own action ends

If the valve does not close, water is running to the neighbour or the damage is behind a wall, it is not worth experimenting. Report to the building manager if it is part of the building, or book an emergency plumber with the form. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
