602-series bathroom - the plumbing plan before a renovation

A 602-series bathroom in Imanta’s nine-storey blocks is a factory-made monolithic reinforced concrete module 2.55 m to the ceiling, so in a renovation there is not much you can move in the walls and floor.

Before choosing tiles, measure three things: the distance from the toilet and shower to the drain, how many centimetres the floor can rise, and where the hatch will be at the connections. The same measurements apply in a five-storey 464-series block.

A concrete cabin and a wall you do not chisel

The study of the 602 series commissioned by the Ministry of Economics (2022) says that the sanitary cabins are three-dimensional, of monolithic reinforced concrete, and made in the factory as a single module. Data from the same study: floor-to-floor storey height 2.70 m, floor to ceiling in a standard storey 2.55 m. Every centimetre the floor rises for the fall comes out of these 2.55 m.

The study also states that the internal load-bearing cross wall between the corridor and the room along the sanitary room is a ventilation block panel. Grooves or openings must not be chiselled into it at will, so for new pipes this wall is not the way.

The Swedish industry rules Säker Vatten are not a Latvian standard. Here they are an example, and their figures help to see where a plan becomes risky.

Toilet next to the riser, the rest further down

Under Säker Vatten the toilet outlet can run straight to the riser, but the sink, bath and shower pipes are connected further down with a branch at an angle of no more than 45°. This way it is harder for the toilet’s water to flow back into other outlets. In a small 602-series bathroom/WC unit this means the toilet stays nearer the riser, while the shower and sink can be further away.

Under the same rules, a concealed cistern with a flush pipe must be placed in a watertight location; if it is fitted in a factory-waterproofed structure, its joint with the wall waterproofing must be checked. For repairing the frame and cistern later, read concealed cistern repair.

In Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulations No. 907 the average service life for drain pipelines with cast iron fittings is 45 years, while Imanta’s buildings were mainly built in 1967–1975. If the riser section behind your toilet is original, decide on replacing it before the tiles; for how a section is replaced, see the page riser replacement.

Combining the bathroom and the toilet

If you want to combine the rooms, start with the documents, not the wall. The Riga Building Board has explained on the LV portal that to connect a room with the bathroom, the layout must be drawn for the whole flat, not only the rebuilt part. Which procedure applies to the partition between the toilet and the bathroom in your building, ask the building board and the building manager.

If you are buying a flat where the bathroom/WC unit has already been combined, the building board advises in the LV portal’s e-consultation asking the seller for the current cadastral survey file and comparing it with the real layout. It does not give full certainty; the only way to check that building work was lawful is a submission to the building board.

In a combined room everything is in one wet zone. Säker Vatten requires a floor drain in rooms with a bath, shower or shower enclosure, and with a washing machine or appliances of 5 l or more that are meant to be emptied, for example a water heater.

What residents ask

In a 602-series bathroom can the toilet be moved to the opposite wall?

It depends on the distance to the riser and on how much the floor can be raised. Grooves must not be chiselled into the ventilation block wall. Before deciding, measure the distance and check where the old pipe runs.

Why must a drain pipe in the wall be wrapped in insulation?

So that it does not touch the concrete directly. Pipelife states that the insulation separates the pipe from the structure and reduces the transmission of noise.

Is the bathroom in a 602-series block built in 1984 the same as in a 1970s block?

Not always. The Ministry of Economics study mentions that the 1984 project corrections developed new types of industrial sanitary blocks. Before planning, open the old finish in one place and see what is behind it.
