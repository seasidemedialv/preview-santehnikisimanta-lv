In a panel-block flat the job is shaped by two things: what will stand under the sink (a cabinet, a machine hose or nothing) and what kind of wall the sink will hang on.

Where the sink hangs: 602, 464 and 119 series

A sink in a 602-series block by the ventilation block panel

A Ministry of Economics study of the 602 series says that the wall between the flat’s hallway and the room along the bathroom is a ventilation block panel. Chases or openings must not be cut into it at will. So the pipes to the sink here run along the wall surface or along another wall, not chased in.

A sink in the gypsum-concrete partition of a grey 464-series block

In five-storey 464-series blocks the partitions are made of gypsum concrete. For the fischer WST II sink set, full gypsum panels are also named as suitable substrates, but tiles and plaster are not a load-bearing base. The DuoPower anchor needs a 12 mm hole at least 80 mm deep, and this depth is counted in the wall behind the tile.

A sink on a new plasterboard wall in a 119-series flat in Zolitūde

The 119 series is known for allowing a flat to be rebuilt extensively without fear of taking down partition walls, so after a rebuild the sink can end up on a new plasterboard wall. Knauf’s technical information says that the load-bearing frame for a sink must withstand a breaking load of 1.5 kN (DIN EN 14688), so the sink is fixed to the frame or a cross-member, not only to the board.

Installing a kitchen sink in a worktop: for one model Blanco gives a cut-out about 10 mm smaller than the outer perimeter of the sink. Before buying, measure the old cut-out and the cabinet.

Tightening no harder than 5 Nm

The installation instructions for the Laufen Ino wall-hung basin require all the fixing holes to be used and the fixings tightened no harder than 5 Nm. For a ceramic basin this means tightening evenly to the stated figure, not all the way with force. For other models the figure may differ, and it is given in the instructions.

For a kitchen sink Blanco asks for similar care: the fixing clamps are tightened in turn and not over-tightened. The weight of the sink is carried by the worktop, and the clamps only press it down.

What changes the sum: the pipe to the riser

Sink installation from €40 each includes fixing, and connecting the trap and the tap. The sum changes most with the drain pipe from the sink to the riser. If the old pipe is replaced, it is a separate line: replacing a drain pipe in a flat from €30 per connection point.

For example: in a 464-series bathroom a new sink is fitted and its drain pipe to the riser is replaced at the same time. Sink installation from €40 and pipe replacement from €30 together give at least €70. The price is for the work, materials extra. If the way to you is charged separately, you learn it on the call, before the sink is removed. Other lines are on the prices page.

What residents ask

The holes of the new sink do not match the old ones. What is done?

The holes are marked from the new sink. The fischer DuoPower anchor needs a 12 mm hole at least 80 mm deep, and it reaches full load capacity only if the screw is driven in to at least the minimum depth. So the new hole needs solid wall over this whole depth.

In a 602-series block, may pipes to the sink be chased into the wall?

Not in the wall between the hallway and the room along the bathroom: it is a ventilation block panel, and chases or openings must not be cut in it at will. For other walls, ask the building manager. Planning is described on the page about a bathroom in a 602-series block.

What has to be fitted on a kitchen sink before it goes into the worktop?

Blanco advises fitting the mixer tap, the drain strainer and the accessories on the sink while it is still outside. A strip of silicone is applied under the edge all the way round. If the cut-out is not properly sealed, the manufacturer takes no responsibility for water damage.
