On roots TEPPFA adds that a mechanical root cutter is only a temporary solution, because the roots grow back.

Grease in the kitchen, roots in the yard, a new network in the street

A kitchen sink on the 5th floor of a 602-series block

Each flat usually has its own branch to the riser; the latest Soviet standard SNiP 2.04.01-85 required this, forbidding the fixtures of different flats on one floor from being connected to one branch. So a layer of grease in your kitchen branch usually comes from your own sink. A drain snake clears it as far as the riser, but the layer forms again if grease keeps going into the sink.

A tree over the line in Beberbeķi

Before the network construction works, Rīgas ūdens sent Beberbeķi and Imanta residents diagrams with the branch positions to every property and asked them to check whether trees or structures would hinder the connection. If a tree nevertheless stayed over the yard outlet, roots can grow into the pipe. TEPPFA states that in the long term only replacing the section or removing the tree helps; more on that on the page waste pipe replacement.

The 2nd Imanta line after connection to the city network

In phase 6 Rīgas ūdens built sewerage in the 1st, 2nd, 3rd and 4th Imanta lines, and in August 2024 announced that the works were finished and connection was possible. For many houses on these streets the yard outlet to the city network is new. Whether it can be cleared with a drain snake depends on the diameter and depth: TEPPFA considers a drain snake suitable for pipes up to 250 mm that lie no deeper than 2 m.

A drum machine: one person, water and several passes

For its K-400 drum machine RIDGID requires that the same person operates the cable and the foot switch. If two people do it, the cable can twist, kink and snap. The cable must not be pulled out of the drain while it is still turning, because its end can whip back and cause serious injury.

To keep the branch clean along its whole length, RIDGID advises running water into the drain during cleaning, which flushes away the loosened dirt, watching the water level and running the line several times. In a short kitchen branch that means: after the first pass the water may already drain, but the grease on the pipe walls still stays.

If the branch has to be replaced after cleaning

In a flat, what changes the price most is whether cleaning is enough. Clearing a blockage is from €50 per call-out. If cleaning shows that the branch pipe itself is damaged, the row “Waste pipe replacement in a flat”, from €30 per connection point, is added.

For example: in a 464-series block a kitchen branch has clogged, and after cleaning the old pipe to the riser has to be replaced. Cleaning at least €50 and replacing the pipe at one connection point at least €30, together at least €80, labour only, materials extra. Whether travel costs extra, you learn on the first call, before the drain snake goes into the pipe.

What residents ask

The riser has clogged in a nine-storey block. Where are its cleanouts?

The latest SNiP 2.04.01-85 required cleanouts in the riser at least every three floors in buildings of 5 or more floors. If your building has them, the nearest may be in a neighbour’s flat; the building manager shows where they are, and he usually arranges riser cleaning.

A flat upstairs keeps blocking the riser. Who should the complaint go to?

A lawyer at the LV portal pointed out in 2025 that if the occupant of an upper flat often blocks the sewer, complaints must be directed to the flat owner. The reason is Section 10(1) point 6 of the Law on Residential Property, which requires careful treatment of common property. In a specific case, ask the building manager.

A house in the Imanta lines still has a holding tank. Is it cleaned with a drain snake?

No, a holding tank is emptied by a cesspit emptying contractor, and Rīgas ūdens states that a vacuum tanker must be able to reach it. Rīgas ūdens also reminded owners of houses in the Imanta lines: if there is a centralised sewer in the street by the property, the connection had to be made by 31 December 2023.
