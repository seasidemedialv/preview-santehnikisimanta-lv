Here you can see what to look for at the toilet and the sink, how a five-storey block, a nine-storey block and a private house differ, and which price rows apply to your job.

What you can notice in the toilet and at the sink

Water drips behind the toilet only when you press flush
the flexible connector is often to blame, as its material hardens with time and no longer seals.
The cistern fills so loudly that you hear it in the next room
according to Geberit, the Type 383 fill valve is half as noisy as Type 380.
The sink wobbles on a new plasterboard wall
Knauf allows only about 40 kg per metre of wall length for its light partitions without an extra crossbar.
The toilet or the sink dates from the first years of the building
in Cabinet Regulations No. 907, the average service life of ceramic toilets and ceramic sinks is 30 years.

If the toilet leaks or runs on without stopping, start with concealed cistern repair or toilet installation, depending on whether the cistern is in the wall or on the toilet.

Service life: Annex 2 of Cabinet Regulations No. 907 (likumi.lv), checked on 17.11.2026.

The toilet and bathroom in three different buildings

Type of building What to know What to check
Grey five-storey 464-series block The partition walls are gypsum concrete. The Ministry of Economics study of the 464 series found flats where rooms were merged in renovations. What is behind the tile where the sink or cistern will be fixed, and whether an earlier renovation connected the toilet to the bathroom.
White nine-storey 602-series block The bathroom/WC units are monolithic reinforced-concrete modules, made in a factory as a single piece. Whether the new toilet and cistern fit in the existing toilet room, and where access to the riser must stay in the unit.
Private house in Kleisti, Beberbeķi or Šampēteris Rīgas ūdens states that the owner is responsible for the water supply and drains from the ownership boundary to the building and inside the house. Where the drain pipe leaves the house and whether the new fixture has enough fall to reach it.

In a multi-flat building the riser is common property. If the work touches the riser itself, not just a branch in the flat, ask the building manager: Article 13 of the Flat Property Law provides that such costs are usually covered under a decision of the flat owners, unless the building’s documents say otherwise.

Bathroom plumbing in a few square metres

In the German planning guide Baunetz Wissen, a toilet with a concealed cistern is allowed about 40 cm in width and 60 cm in depth, and a shower tray starts from 80 × 80 cm. For a single sink it counts at least 60 cm in width and 55 cm in depth. Between the bath and the opposite wall it recommends at least 55 cm, ideally 105 cm.

These are not Latvian standards, but with a tape measure you can check whether the new set will fit in the old room. If a shower replaces the bath, see shower enclosure installation. For a washing machine in a small bathroom, the space and supply pipes are described in washing machine connection, and new outlets before tiling in moving plumbing outlets.

A cistern in the wall without major rebuilding

The Geberit Monolith module combines the flushing mechanism, the water supply and the drain outlet in one housing. The manufacturer writes that it can be installed on the existing connections without opening the wall. That is an option if you do not want to chisel into the walls of the toilet room.

For a masonry wall, Geberit offers a Kombifix element with a Sigma 8 cm cistern: the brackets fit 8 or 12 cm thick walls, and the set includes a Ø 90 mm connection bend with a reducer to 110 mm. Viega, for its part, makes an auxiliary frame that lets a Prevista element be installed in a room corner.

What happens to the cistern after some years is described on the page about concealed cistern repair.

An example with two jobs in one visit

For example: in a five-storey 464-series flat, an old floor-standing toilet and the sink in the bathroom have to be replaced, while the supply pipes and drains stay where they are. Two rows apply: Toilet replacement from €50 and Sink installation from €40, together at least €90.

If the tap on the sink is replaced at the same time, Mixer tap replacement from €25 is added, and the labour sum is at least €115. That is labour only, materials extra.

What the sum depends on

  • Whether the supply pipes and drains stay in the old places: a new place is a separate row, New water or drain outlet from €55.
  • Whether the drain pipe to the riser is sound: if it is replaced, Replacing a drain pipe in a flat from €30 applies.
  • What kind of wall the sink or frame is fixed to, and whether extra fixing is needed.
  • Whether the toilet is on the floor or on a frame in the wall: Concealed toilet frame with toilet from €85, wall finishing extra.

The plumber says on the call, before the work starts, whether travel has its own charge. You also go over the job and the price on that same call, so in the form it is worth writing what is being changed and in which room. All the rows are on the prices page.

What residents ask

Can a toilet, a sink and a tap be installed in one visit?

Yes. Each job has its own price row, and the rows are added together. If there are several small jobs, for example replacing a trap and tightening a joint, the Hourly rate for small jobs from €25/h can work out cheaper, see plumber by the hour.

What has to be decided before the tiler starts?

Where the toilet, sink and shower supply pipes and drains will be, and whether the cistern will be in the wall. New outlets are built before the walls are finished, while the toilet itself is connected to the frame after tiling.

Is the work in a private house in Bieriņi different from a flat?

The house has no shared riser with neighbours, and everything from the ownership boundary to the house and inside it is the owner’s responsibility. On the other hand, you need to check that the drain from the new fixture has enough fall to reach the house outlet.

How do I order the work if I do not know the name of the part I need?

Describe in the form what you see and in which room, for example “leaking under the sink cabinet”. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and on the call you find out what it is and what it costs.
