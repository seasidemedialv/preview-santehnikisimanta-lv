The unit matters most here. Three fixtures are three points, while ten metres of pipe along a wall are ten metres. The riser is common property, so replacing it usually needs a decision of the flat owners or the building manager’s approval; ask the building manager. More on the page riser replacement.

What residents ask

The Imanta lines now have the city sewer. How big is the co-financing for the connection pipe?

Under Riga City Council binding regulations No. RD-23-207-sn, the co-financing for a sewer connection depends on the length of the connection pipe: up to 5 m €1,000, 5–20 m €1,500, over 20 m €2,000. It is granted after the connection is built, and it is not provided for a water supply connection. This price page covers unblocking and inspecting an existing outlet, not building a connection.

Why does the water heater descaling row start higher than water heater installation?

Descaling is from €75 per item and installation or replacement from €70 per item. In descaling the water heater is drained, the limescale is removed, and the anode and heating element are checked, while installation connects the new water heater to the existing water pipe with a safety group. For what to choose for an old water heater, see the page water heater descaling.

In summer there is no hot water for one day in Imanta. Is that a case for the water heater row?

Usually not. In summer AS “Rīgas siltums” runs hydraulic tests on the heating network zone by zone: a test lasts one day, and if no faults are found, hot water returns the same evening. Building managers are informed about it. If you still want your own water heater in the flat, the row “Water heater installation or replacement”, from €70 per item, applies.
