Water heater descaling - limescale in the tank and on the heating element

Water heater descaling in Imanta means draining the tank, removing limescale and sludge from the bottom, and checking the anode and the heating element.

In 2025 Rīgas ūdens water averaged 49 mg/L of calcium and 13 mg/L of magnesium, and when heated these settle in the tank as limescale. Water heater descaling costs from €75 per item.

When limescale has built up in the tank

  • While heating you hear hissing or crackling in the tank.
  • The water heater takes longer to heat than before, although the thermostat has not been changed.
  • On a steatite model the safety cut-out trips: in that case Atlantic advises first cleaning the heating element sheath, not replacing the element.
  • When you open the hot tap, white grains or flakes come with the water.

If the hot water flow is weak, start with the tap. Stiebel Eltron names a limescaled or dirty tap aerator or shower head as a cause, and these are cleaned or descaled without a plumber.

What water reaches the water heater in Imanta

Rīgas ūdens data for 2025: average hardness 1.82 mmol/L, alkalinity 2.3 mmol/L, calcium 49 mg/L, magnesium 13 mg/L. Iron (0.11 mg/L) and manganese (0.02 mg/L) are below the limits of Cabinet Regulations No. 547, which are 0.20 and 0.05 mg/L.

Rīgas ūdens explains that over time the salts and minerals in the water settle on the pipes and can come loose when the pressure changes. In Imanta the main water pipes were flushed on schedule in 2020; during flushing the water can be cloudy. What comes loose from the pipes on such days can also end up on the bottom of the water heater.

The labour price for descaling and replacement is close, so what decides is the condition of the tank. A sound tank with limescale is a case for cleaning, a leaking tank is a case for replacement.

Descaling step by step

  1. Power and water. The water heater’s fuse is switched off and the cold water supply is closed.
  2. Draining. The tank is drained so the flange with the heating unit can be removed.
  3. Deposits. Sludge and loose limescale are removed from the bottom of the tank (Stiebel Eltron, Atlantic).
  4. Flange and sheaths. The flange is descaled only after removal (Stiebel Eltron); the heating element and thermostat sheaths are cleaned, also from the inside (Atlantic).
  5. Anode. Its condition is inspected; it is not treated with a descaler.
  6. Reassembly. With a new gasket, tightening the nuts gradually and crosswise (Atlantic).
  7. Refilling. Stiebel Eltron says to fill the water heater first and only then switch the power on; the next day the gasket is checked again (Atlantic).

Water heater anode replacement and anti-corrosion parts

The anode protects the tank from corrosion, which is why it is inspected during descaling. Stiebel Eltron forbids treating the tank surface and the protective anode with descalers. In the same manufacturer’s PSH Classic model there is an anti-corrosion resistor (560 Ω), which must not be damaged or removed during servicing. The Atlantic floor-standing model with a steatite element has an ACI anode. If the anode has to be replaced, the new anode counts as materials and is charged extra.

A water softener and the water heater

Water that is too soft does not suit a water heater either. The Ariston Andris Elite water heater must not be fed water with a hardness below 120 ppm or above 250 ppm, and the hardness remaining after a softener must stay at least 150 ppm. Atlantic requires the softener to be set correctly and the hardness to stay above 8 °f (French degrees).

Thermex claims that the InoxDryHeat dry heating element of the Mara Pro series prevents limescale forming on the element. That applies to the element; deposits on the tank bottom are a separate matter.

The price changes with the water heater volume, the amount of limescale, whether the water heater has to be taken off the wall, and whether after inspection a new heating element or thermostat is needed. What residents ask

Can I descale the water heater myself with vinegar?

Stiebel Eltron forbids treating the tank surface and the protective anode with descalers; only a removed flange is descaled. If you work yourself, switch off the power and fill the water heater before switching on.

The fuse trips when the water heater starts to heat. Will cleaning help?

For a steatite model Atlantic advises first cleaning the heating element sheath with a rag or a bottle brush and replacing the element only if the problem stays. For a model with an immersed heating element the element is replaced in that case, which is done in water heater repair.

Will the flat be without water during cleaning?

Only the water heater’s cold water supply is closed, so the other taps work if the flat has a separate valve before the water heater. If there is none, the flat’s incoming supply is closed for the time of the work.
