Where to turn the water off while the plumber is on the way

  1. 1

    Under the sink

    The small valves on the hoses close only that tap; the rest of the flat keeps its water.

  2. 2

    At the flat's inlet

    The valve before the water meter, usually in the bathroom's pipe box, closes the whole flat.

  3. 3

    On the riser in the basement

    If the riser itself is leaking, the building manager's emergency service closes it.

Plumbing help also starts at a tap, a trap, a valve or a water heater: describe in the form what has happened, and on the call you go over the job and the price.

From the 1st to the 9th floor on one riser

In one Imanta panel block, water, drains and heat behave differently on the 1st and the 9th floor, even though the pipes are the same. The manufacturer Wilo explains that water pressure falls both because of height, from the building’s incoming supply to the highest tap, and because of friction, and in old pipes corrosion and deposits increase the friction.

The cleanouts on a stack are not random. The Soviet standard SNiP 2.04.01-85, which later panel blocks were designed to, required them in buildings of 5 storeys or more at least every three storeys.

For pressure zones in multi-storey buildings, the manufacturer Grundfos gives a target: on the top floor of a zone the pressure should not fall below 1.5–2 bar, and on the lowest it should not exceed 4–4.5 bar. So a weak shower on the 9th floor can be a whole-building question, while on the 1st floor the same tap gets the highest pressure in the building. If the flow is weak in every flat, the LV portal advises reporting to the building manager first and writing to the water company only after the internal pipes have been checked. More: weak pressure on upper floors.

Drains are the other way round: the problem is seen first at the bottom. SNiP 2.04.01-85 forbade connecting fixtures of different flats on one floor to the same branch pipe, so each flat has its own connection to the riser. If the stack is blocked below the branch of the 1st or 2nd floor, water from the floors above has nowhere to go. It appears in the lowest flat’s sink or bath, even though that flat’s trap is not at fault.

Heat travels along the same vertical. The LV portal explains that post-war multi-flat buildings mostly have one-pipe vertical heating systems: radiators receive heat one after another, and the radiator bypass keeps a constant flow in the riser. That is why a radiator on one floor is not replaced without a bypass, as it also affects the other floors on the same riser. More: radiator replacement.

Kitchen: sink, trap and machine connection

In the kitchen the problem is usually seen in the cupboard under the sink: a trap nut drips, a tap hose leaks or water drains slowly. The Swedish industry rules Säker Vatten state that a third of water damage in flats happens in the kitchen, mainly from appliances connected to water, such as dishwashers.

If water rises in the sink when the neighbours upstairs wash up, the blockage is usually in the shared kitchen riser, not in your trap. Sink installation costs from €40, connecting a washing machine or dishwasher from €35, clearing a blockage in a flat from €50. More: sink installation, washing machine connection and drain unblocking.

Bathroom: tap, bath and shower

In the appendix to Cabinet Regulation No. 907, the average service life of bathroom taps, ceramic basins and toilets is 30 years, and of brass and cast-iron valves 40 years. Most Imanta panel blocks are more than 50 years old, so an original tap or valve that has not yet been replaced has already passed that age.

Replacing a mixer tap costs from €25, replacing a bath from €60, installing a shower enclosure from €125. If a basin or shower is moved during a renovation, the new water or drain outlet is made before tiling, and it costs from €55 per outlet. More: mixer tap replacement, shower enclosure installation, moving plumbing outlets and the advice on a bathroom in a 602-series flat.

Toilet: bowl, cistern and frame

In the toilet a fault is more often heard than seen: the cistern keeps filling after a flush, or a thin stream runs into the bowl all the time. Usually the fill valve or the flush valve is at fault, and replacing the flush mechanism costs from €35, also in a concealed cistern.

If the toilet is connected to an old cast-iron drain, replacing it takes more work: in a Russian-language guide at t-j.ru a plumber notes that in older housing with cast-iron pipes a toilet is harder to install. Replacing a floor-standing toilet costs from €50, a concealed frame with a toilet from €85. More: toilet installation and concealed cistern repair.

Heat and hot water

In panel blocks, hot water and heating are supplied by Rīgas siltums. In summer it carries out hydraulic tests of the heating networks zone by zone: a test lasts one day, and if no faults are found, hot water is restored the same evening. The company informs the building managers about interruptions. In February 2026 a 49 MW electrode water boiler was installed at the Imanta heating plant, and the company stated that in summer it could supply hot water to the whole of Imanta.

In a private house, hot water usually comes from the water heater itself. Installing or replacing a water heater costs from €70, descaling from €75. Replacing a radiator (from €75) and a heated towel rail (from €80) in a multi-flat building follows the procedure of Rīgas siltums: a designer prepares the documents and they are agreed with the building manager. The working window is roughly between 1 May and 1 October, when the riser in question can be drained. More: water heaters, heating repair and radiator replacement.

Riser and basement

Replacing a riser section in a flat usually needs a decision of the flat owners or the building manager’s approval; ask the building manager. Once that is in place, one floor’s section costs from €110 for a water riser and from €100 for a soil stack.

In the basement, SNiP 2.04.01-85 provided a valve and a drain plug at the foot of each riser, so that the riser could be shut off and drained for repair. Cabinet Regulation No. 907 also requires the basement inspection to check whether there is condensation on the water pipes. More: riser replacement, pipe replacement and an emergency plumber if it is leaking now.

The building’s incoming supply pipes run under the yard asphalt. On 28 March 2023 a water main burst in the inner yard at Kurzemes prospekts 38, and a car drove into the hole it washed out. If a depression appears in the yard, report it to the building manager at once, and in the flat check whether the water pressure or colour has changed.

Streets of detached houses and neighbouring districts

In Imanta the panel blocks stand between Kurzemes prospekts and Anniņmuižas bulvāris, while to the south-west, along the Jūrmala railway and on both sides of Kooperatīva iela, there are detached houses with their own incoming supply. Street water mains are being renewed in these streets. On 26 August 2025 Rīgas ūdens signed a contract for renewing a water main by the pipe-bursting method, including on Dammes, Kooperatīva and Raktes streets, and on 15 June 2026 it announced the same procurement for Slokas iela.

The pipe-bursting method renews the street main without an open trench, but the pipe from the meter chamber to the house and the main valve stay the same. That is the time to check those too.

These prices also apply in Zolitūde, Kleisti, Beberbeķi, Bieriņi and Pleskodāle. Clearing a yard drain costs from €50. For a more distant street, the call before the work settles whether the trip adds a travel cost. All neighbourhoods: plumber in west Riga.

What residents ask

How soon will you contact me after the form?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. On the call you clarify what has happened and agree when the plumber can come.

Can I request a plumber at night or on a Saturday?

Yes, we work also at night and at weekends. Fill in the form and choose “Emergency” in the urgency field.

Who do I report to if a riser leaks in a nine-storey block?

The riser is the building’s common property and is shut off in the basement, so report first to the building manager or their emergency service. If the leak is in the flat’s part behind your own valve, a plumber repairs it; finding and stopping a leak costs from €40.

What should I expect to pay for the first visit?

A visit with fault finding costs from €40, and the hourly rate for small jobs is from €25/h. This is labour only, materials extra. Any extra for travel is mentioned on the call, ahead of the job. All jobs are on the price page.

Can I order several small jobs in one go?

Yes. If you have a list, for example a dripping tap, a trap to tighten and a dishwasher to connect, book a plumber by the hour: from €25/h, and usually at least the first hour is paid.
