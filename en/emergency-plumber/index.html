Where to turn the water off while the plumber is on the way

  1. 1

    Under the sink

    The small valves on the hoses close only that tap; the rest of the flat keeps its water.

  2. 2

    At the flat's inlet

    The valve before the water meter, usually in the bathroom's pipe box, closes the whole flat.

  3. 3

    On the riser in the basement

    If the riser itself is leaking, the building manager's emergency service closes it.

    How to find and close the valves

While you wait, turn off the flat’s shut-off valve (where it is, see the advice page how to shut off water in a flat), switch the water heater off at the fuse and warn the neighbour below. Two questions then remain: where exactly it is leaking if the pipe is not visible, and who is responsible for the neighbour’s ceiling.

Water leaks in Imanta buildings: where they show up

Boxed-in pipes behind the toilet on the 9th floor of a 602-series block

In 602-series blocks the load-bearing walls are expanded-clay concrete panels, the floors are reinforced concrete slabs, and the bathroom/WC unit is a separate room. Water from a leaking connection behind the toilet boxing stays on the slab and looks for a way down along the riser, so a neighbour on the 8th floor often sees it first. If the stain stops growing after you turn off the flat’s shut-off valve, it is the flat’s pipe that is leaking, not the riser.

Soil stack in a five-storey 464-series block

If water comes through the ceiling from a blocked riser, the LV portal explains it like this: the riser is part of the common property, and the flat residents are not at fault in this case. It is different when someone from an upper flat repeatedly blocks the drain. In 2025 a lawyer at the LV portal pointed out that claims are then made against that flat owner. The reason: Article 10(1), point 6 of the Flat Property Law requires care in the use of common property.

Underfloor heating loop in a private house in Kleisti

A pipe under the floor screed cannot be seen, and water may not appear on the surface. If the system is not under pressure or the sound of the leak cannot be heard, the manufacturer SEWERIN recommends tracer gas. A mixture of 5% hydrogen and 95% nitrogen is filled into the pipe, and the VARIOTEC 460 device detects hydrogen above the floor from as little as 1 ppm. This way the leak can be located without opening the whole floor.

Once the spot is found, the rest is ordinary repair: a connection within reach is fixed as leak fixing, but if the riser itself leaks, the job is riser replacement.

Why 50 litres a minute and 24 hours

GROHE explains the logic of its threshold: in normal use 50 litres per minute does not flow even when several family members shower at the same time. So at this flow the valve closes by itself, without anyone stepping in.

Small leaks are a different story. Sense Guard checks for them once every 24 hours, and in most cases the check covers only the cold water system; the result is affected by the pipe material and the volume of the system. The same manufacturer notes that after a leak, mould can start to form from the damp within 24 hours, so a damp stain is not worth leaving until the weekend.

Flooded neighbour: who is responsible for the damage

Article 1779 of the Civil Law states that everyone must compensate the damage caused by their action or inaction, for example by flooding a neighbour. Article 1635 gives the injured party the right to claim satisfaction, to the extent that the wrongdoer can be blamed for the action.

Article 14 of the Flat Property Law provides that a flat owner is liable for such damage as laid down by law, the decisions of the flat owners or contracts. So if your connection leaks, the flat owner is usually responsible, but for a riser the roles may be divided by the building’s documents. Ask the building manager.

If a flat floods at night, the LV portal’s e-consultation suggests calling the building manager in the morning to record the fact, and photographing everything as evidence.

A flat pipe or a riser section: this sets the amount

One question changes the amount most in emergency work: whether the leak is in the flat’s pipe or in the riser. Finding and fixing a leak in an accessible place is from €40. Replacing a water riser section is from €110 per section on one floor, and usually needs a decision of the flat owners or the building manager’s consent in line with the building’s decisions; ask the building manager.

For example: in a house in Kleisti the hot water pipe under the floor by the bathroom is seeping, and nobody wants to open the floor. Instead, 4 m of new pipe is laid above the finish, up to two connections. Plumber visit with fault finding from €40, laying water supply pipe from €10/m (4 m is at least €40) and two water supply connection points from €35 (at least €70). In total at least €150, labour only, materials extra.

What residents ask

My connection behind the toilet boxing is leaking. Who pays for the neighbour's ceiling?

Under Article 14 of the Flat Property Law, a flat owner is liable to others for damage as set by law, the decisions of the owners or contracts. If the flat’s own connection leaks, that is usually the flat owner, but the building manager will tell you the exact procedure in your building.

Will Sense Guard notice a small leak in a hot water pipe?

GROHE states that the micro-leak check runs once every 24 hours and in most cases covers only the cold water system. The valve stops a large burst once the flow reaches 50 litres per minute.

Can tracer gas damage a drinking water pipe?

The SEWERIN mixture is 5% hydrogen and 95% nitrogen. The manufacturer states it is non-toxic and non-corrosive, so it is intended for drinking water pipes and underfloor heating too.
