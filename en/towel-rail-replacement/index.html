The manufacturer Sunerzha connects it in parallel to the main hot water or heating line. It is not responsible for a lack of circulation if it is caused by an installation error or the pipe layout. So the rail is chosen by pressure, temperature and connection type, not only by size.

Towel rails in Imanta bathrooms

Stainless rail on the hot water riser on the 6th floor of a 602-series block
Sunerzha ВПС models are made of AISI 304 L steel, working pressure up to 15 atm, test pressure 25 atm, up to 95 °C. If the system pressure is higher than 15 atm, the manufacturer requires a pressure reducer before the rail. In a nine-storey block this riser runs through all the bathrooms in the vertical, so the building manager sets the working day.
A rail that is cold while the neighbour’s is warm
One possible cause is that water does not circulate through your rail. Sunerzha names this case explicitly: a lack of circulation because of the installation or the pipe layout is not the manufacturer’s responsibility. For the new rail the supply pipes are chosen so that the connection is parallel.
A Kermi towel radiator in the heating loop of a Bieriņi house
The Kermi Basic-50 also suits a one-pipe system, working pressure up to 10 bar, hot water up to 110 °C. At 75/65/20 °C it gives 451 W, at 55/45/20 °C only 240 W, so in a low-temperature loop it gives about half the heat.

Sunerzha states that in a multi-flat building the rail is installed with the consent of the building management service and then checked by drawing up an acceptance certificate; for the procedure in your building, ask the building manager.

Potential equalisation and 5 kg

A water-fed rail is a metal pipe in the bathroom. Sunerzha requires the rail to be connected to the supplementary potential equalisation system and forbids earthing electrical appliances through the water pipe. This wire is connected by an electrician.

In use the manufacturer limits the load to 5 kg, and the seals of threaded joints have to be replaced periodically because of impurities in the water. That is why a drip at a nut is first looked for in the seal.

An electric rail without a riser

If you want the rail on another wall or do not want to wait for the riser’s working day, an electric rail is not tied to the riser. It is fixed to the wall, but the electrical connection in the bathroom is made by an electrician. Moving a water-fed rail to another place is a different job from replacing it, and for the procedure ask the building manager.

What a rail replacement costs

One thing changes the price here: whether the new rail’s connection matches the old supply pipes. If it does, it is Towel rail replacement from €80 per item; if a threaded joint still drips afterwards, re-sealing it is Finding and fixing a leak from €40. Labour only, materials extra. If travel is charged separately, you will hear it on the phone call, before the plumber sets off.

For example: in a Bieriņi house the old rail in the heating loop is replaced with a Kermi Basic-50 with a centre connection, and one joint of the old supply pipe is re-sealed. That is at least €120 of work.

What residents ask

Can a heavy wet blanket be dried on the rail?

Sunerzha limits the load on the rail to 5 kg. A wet blanket can be heavier, so it is better dried elsewhere.

When does a rail need a reducer?

Sunerzha requires a reducer if the pressure in the hot water or heating system exceeds 15 atm, so that it is not higher than 15 atm at the rail.

Can an electric heating element be added to a Kermi rail later?

The Kermi Basic-50 allows it only with a centre connection. With a side connection the T-piece that is needed changes the connection point, so the heating element cannot be added later.
