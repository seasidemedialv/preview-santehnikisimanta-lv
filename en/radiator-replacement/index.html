Radiators that land on the workbench in Imanta

A cast iron MC-140-500 under the window in a 464-series room
Under the GOST 8690-94 designation, the number 140 is the section depth and 500 is the distance between the nipple opening centres in millimetres. If the connections of the new radiator do not match the old supply pipes, IMI Heimeier offers S-shaped connectors that make up the difference, and reminds you to observe the flow direction.
A bimetal radiator on the 9th floor of a 602-series block
The LV portal explains that post-war apartment blocks mostly have a one-pipe vertical system: radiators receive heat in sequence, and the flow in the riser is maintained by a bypass line. With the IMI Heimeier E-Z System manifold in one-pipe mode the bypass stays open even if the radiator is shut off, and 30–60% of the flow (35% at the factory) can be directed to the radiator.
A bathroom radiator in a house in Šampēteris with a low-temperature circuit
In a Kermi table, the bathroom radiator Basic-50 gives 451 W at 75/65/20 °C, but only 240 W at 55/45/20 °C. If the building’s heat source runs at a lower temperature, the radiator must be chosen larger, not the same as the old one.

In an apartment block, before the replacement you need the designer’s documentation with a heat loss calculation and the building manager’s approval. Rīgas siltums gives its working period as roughly from 1 May to 1 October; more on heating in the building on the page heating repair in Imanta.

RIFAR prohibits using its radiator in a hot water circuit, including in place of a towel rail; for a bathroom there is heated towel rail replacement.

Height under the windowsill and two shut-off ends

The RIFAR Base instructions require the radiator to be installed so that the heat carrier inlet and outlet can be shut off separately. For better heat output the manufacturer recommends 70–120 mm to the floor, at least 80 mm to the windowsill and 30–50 mm to the wall.

In a panel block the space under the window is limited by the windowsill and the existing supply pipes. So before buying, measure the height from the floor to the windowsill. If it is 600 mm, for a radiator that is 500 mm high only 100 mm remains for both clearances together, while the RIFAR recommendation requires at least 70 + 80 = 150 mm. Then a lower model must be chosen.

Replacement per unit

For this job the price is set by the number of radiators. Radiator replacement is from €75 each, if the new radiator connects to the existing supply pipes and the riser is shut off and refilled by the building manager or the house owner. The design and approval are not part of the plumber’s labour price. Labour only, materials extra. The plumber names any travel charge on the first call.

For example: in a three-room 464-series flat three cast iron radiators are replaced with steel panels at the same supply pipes. That is at least €225 of work (3 × from €75), materials extra.

What residents ask

How much output does a radiator lose if the circuit runs at 55/45 °C?

In a Kermi table the bathroom radiator Basic-50 gives 451 W at 75/65/20 °C and 240 W at 55/45/20 °C, so about half. A radiator is chosen by the temperature at which your heat source runs.

The neighbour upstairs has already replaced a radiator without a bypass. What should I do?

In a one-pipe riser the flow is maintained by a bypass line, so a radiator without one can affect the heat in the riser. Tell the building manager about it; in your own flat leave the bypass in place.

Can sections be added to a bimetal radiator later?

RIFAR does not recommend reassembling its Base radiator, nor replacing individual sections. The length is chosen at the start.
