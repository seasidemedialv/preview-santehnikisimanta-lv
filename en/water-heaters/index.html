EU Regulation No. 814/2013 requires at least 65 litres of mixed water at 40 °C from a water heater with load profile M, and at least 130 litres with profile L. In a panel-block flat a water heater is usually a backup for the building’s hot water, in a private house it often heats everything; installation or replacement from €70 each.

Three water heaters in Imanta and nearby

A backup water heater in a 602-series flat

The hot water in Imanta’s buildings comes from the network of AS “Rīgas siltums”. In February 2026 a 49 MW electrode boiler was installed at the Imanta heating plant, and the company said that in summer it could supply hot water to the whole of Imanta. A flat’s water heater does not depend on it, because it heats from electricity. If you need a water heater only as a backup, you can consider a smaller one: for profile S the regulation allows a volume of up to 36 litres.

A small water heater at the kitchen sink in a 464-series block

Atlantic advises placing a water heater as close as possible to where the most water is drawn, that is, the bathroom or the kitchen. If hot water is needed only for dishes, a very small water heater will do: for profiles XXS and XS the regulation limits the volume to 15 litres.

All the hot water of a private house in Beberbeķi

If all your hot water comes from the water heater, look at profile L or XL on the label. For profile XL the regulation requires at least 210 litres of mixed water at 40 °C, and since 26.09.2017 the minimum water heating energy efficiency for profile L and XL water heaters is 37%. In Beberbeķi, in stage 5, Rīgas ūdens built 11.88 km of water main, which allows about 150 households to connect.

The label class and standing heat loss

The class of a hot water tank, from A+ to G, is set by how many watts it loses on standby, taking the volume into account (Regulation No. 812/2013). Since 26.09.2017 these losses must not exceed 16.66 + 8.33 × V^0.4 watts, where V is the volume in litres. If two water heaters have the same volume and profile, lower standby losses mean a higher class.

Annexes II to Regulations (EU) No. 812/2013 and 814/2013, checked 17.11.2026.

Riga's water hardness and the type of heating element

Rīgas ūdens data show that the average hardness of drinking water in 2025 was 1.82 mmol/L, with a calcium content of 49 mg/L and a magnesium content of 13 mg/L. That is the limescale which after some years stays on the bottom of the tank and on the heating element. Some manufacturers set limits in the other direction too: Ariston does not allow water with a hardness below 120 ppm to be fed to its Andris Elite water heater, and if a softener is installed, the remaining hardness must not fall below 150 ppm.

The type of heating element changes both where the limescale builds up and the repair. In the Atlantic steatite model the ceramic element sits in an enamelled protective sleeve, so it is replaced without draining the water heater. Thermex claims that the dry heating element InoxDryHeat in the Mara Pro series prevents scale forming on the element. An immersed element sits directly in the water, and it is descaled during water heater descaling.

What residents ask

Why does a new water heater give less hot water than the old one?

Atlantic explains that the tank used to store water at 80 °C, but for safety it is now around 65 °C. So a water heater of the same volume can yield less hot water; compare the load profile, not only the litres.

Can a Thermex flat water heater be hung lying down?

Thermex states that Mara Pro series flat water heaters with two tanks can be hung both vertically and horizontally. This does not apply to all: the Ariston Andris Elite works only vertically.
