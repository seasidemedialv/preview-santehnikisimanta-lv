Privacy policy

This page explains what data the website santehnikisimanta.lv receives and who it is passed to.

The data is received by the service provider, that is, the plumber who answers your request.

What data we receive

Only what you enter in the request form:

  • name;
  • phone number;
  • neighbourhood;
  • address;
  • type of problem;
  • urgency;
  • description, if you fill it in.

Who the data goes to and why

After sending, the request goes to a request-handling system, from which the service provider receives it. The data is used only to contact you on the phone number you gave and to go over the requested work. The basis for processing is your request to be contacted before a possible contract.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you can ask for access to your data, and for it to be corrected or deleted. To do this, send a request with the form on the contact us page and write “Data request” in the description. If you believe the data has been processed incorrectly, you can turn to the Data State Inspectorate.

Sources used
